See the Shake

Film a running machine in slow motion. See how each part vibrates, watch the shake magnified, and get the likely fault.

With Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 on, Spark names the parts to measure and SAM 3.1 masks them; any masks or boxes you add are the fallback if SAM finds nothing. With them off, mark the parts yourself. Prefer parts that do not rotate: measure a fan through its motor housing, not its blades. A full run costs a few cents. Phones often save slow motion as a slowed 30 fps file: if the clip was filmed at 240 fps, enter 240 under Captured at.
