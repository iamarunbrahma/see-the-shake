See the Shake

Film a running machine in slow motion. See how each part vibrates, watch the shake magnified, and get the likely fault.

The twin is a rendered washing machine with a known fault, camera shake, light flicker and sensor noise. Its exact part masks stand in for SAM 3.1, and the true running speed plus or minus 10% stands in for the Muse Spark plan. Results are compared with the truth.

Until SAM 3.1 is connected, mark the parts yourself with a mask or a box. Prefer parts that do not rotate: measure a fan through its motor housing, not its blades.
