### Would break on a real clip

1. **Compression artifacts inflate velocity, and the alert level depends on it.** The phone encoding adds peaks
   that are not machine motion: a comb at multiples of 20 Hz in y (20, 40, 60, 80 Hz, likely the encoder's
   frame-type cadence at 240 fps / 12) and mixing products in x (63, 77, 97 Hz). Velocity weights each peak by
   its frequency, so these carry about 70% of the healthy run's velocity squared: 6.1 mm/s against an alert
   level of 7.0. A real healthy machine filmed this way could be reported as a fault from compression alone.
   Possible fixes (in `measure.py` / `fingerprint.py`): compute velocity from running-speed orders only, or label
   peaks on an encoder cadence comb the way light flicker is labelled, using the background part to find it.
2. **Small motions read low.** Default encoder tuning keeps 64% of the healthy 1x (0.03 mm) and 82 to 103% of
   the larger ones. The twin's 20% amplitude target holds for the near-lossless encoding, not for phone files.
   Real-clip amplitudes should be reported as lower bounds.
3. **The noise floor doubles** (about 0.0007 mm here). The sub-pixel tracking still separates every fault, but
   the margin is thinner on the real 720p clips, where the machine also spans fewer pixels than in the twin.
4. **Rotation metadata.** Phones save portrait clips as landscape plus a rotation tag. ffmpeg (the SAM sub-clip
   and Spark preview) and OpenCV (measurement) both apply it, so masks and frames agree; a test covers this.
   ffprobe's width and height are the stored, unrotated size, so a mask canvas built from `ClipInfo` would be
   transposed. `segment_parts` now takes its shape from the decoded sub-clip and checks it against the frame
   size SAM reports, failing with a clear message on a mismatch.
5. **Unknowns that only Arun's clips can settle:** the Redmi's real bitrate and its hardware encoder's cadence
   (24 Mbit/s and x264 are assumptions), and whether MIUI saves the clip at 240 fps or slowed to 30 fps (ingest's
   capture-fps override covers the second case).

### Artifact peaks that still get a label

Two artifact labels remain, both in files this session does not edit:
